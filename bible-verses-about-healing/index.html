Bible verses about healing

These seven passages are the collection the app surfaces under Healing — for illness, for grief, and for the kind of injury that does not show. They are offered as scripture to sit with, not as medical advice.

Every verse below is quoted from the King James Version. In the app the same collection is available in the English Standard Version too, and tapping any verse opens it in the reader in full context — with the chapter around it, the other translation beside it, and Father AI available to explain anything that does not land.

The 7 verses, with their guided prayers

  • “Is any sick among you? let him call for the elders of the church; and let them pray over him, anointing him with oil in the name of the Lord:”
    James 5:14 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Lord, I seek Your healing, both physical and spiritual. Let the prayers of faith restore me, and forgive any sins Iâve committed. Amen.

  • “He healeth the broken in heart, and bindeth up their wounds.”
    Psalms 147:3 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Healer of the brokenhearted, bind up my wounds and mend my heart. Your healing touch restores and renews. Amen.

  • “But he wounded for our transgressions, bruised for our iniquities: the chastisement of our peace upon him; and with his stripes we are healed.”
    Isaiah 53:5 · KJV

    Guided prayer. By Your wounds, Jesus, we are healed. Thank You for bearing our sins and sorrows. Bring healing and peace to my life. Amen.

  • “Heal me, O LORD, and I shall be healed; save me, and I shall be saved: for thou my praise.”
    Jeremiah 17:14 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Heal me, O Lord, and I shall be healed. Save me and I shall be saved, for You are my praise. Bring Your healing into my life. Amen.

  • “Come unto me, all that labour and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest.”
    Matthew 11:28 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Jesus, I come to You weary and burdened. Grant me rest and teach me Your humble ways, that my soul may find rest. Amen.

  • “Who his own self bare our sins in his own body on the tree, that we, being dead to sins, should live unto righteousness: by whose stripes ye were healed.”
    1 Peter 2:24 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Lord Jesus, by Your wounds, we are healed. Help me to live righteously, drawing strength and healing from Your sacrifice. Amen.

  • “Beloved, I wish above all things that thou mayest prosper and be in health, even as thy soul prospereth.”
    3 John 1:2 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Dear God, I pray for good health and prosperity, just as my soul prospers. Guide me in living a life that honors You. Amen.

Reading these in context

A verse lifted out of its chapter can be made to say almost anything. Bible Project is built so that a topic list is a way in to the text rather than a substitute for it: tap a verse and you land in the reader at that exact verse, with the surrounding chapter there to be read.

If a sentence is dense or archaic — and the King James Version has plenty of both — you can ask for a plain-language explanation of that verse without leaving the page, then keep asking follow-up questions in the same thread until the answer actually lands. That is Father AI, and it is the one part of the app that needs a connection.

When you are unwell or grieving and offline

The whole point of carrying both translations on the device is that the moment you need them is rarely the moment you have signal. Reading, keyword search, the topic collections, verse compare and the verse of the day all work with the network off entirely.
