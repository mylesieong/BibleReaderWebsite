Bible verses about encouragement

These nine passages are the collection the app surfaces under Encouragement — the verses to reach for when the work is long, the week has been unkind, or someone else needs something better than advice.

Every verse below is quoted from the King James Version. In the app the same collection is available in the English Standard Version too, and tapping any verse opens it in the reader in full context — with the chapter around it, the other translation beside it, and Father AI available to explain anything that does not land.

The 9 verses, with their guided prayers

  • “I can do all things through Christ which strengtheneth me.”
    Philippians 4:13 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Through You, Jesus, I can face all circumstances with strength. Help me to rely on Your power in every aspect of my life. Amen.

  • “Yea, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil: for thou with me; thy rod and thy staff they comfort me.”
    Psalms 23:4 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Even in the darkest valleys, Lord, I fear no evil, for You are with me. Your guidance and support comfort me. Amen.

  • “The name of the LORD a strong tower: the righteous runneth into it, and is safe.”
    Proverbs 18:10 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Lord, You are my strong tower; I run to You for safety. Keep me secure in Your strength and love. Amen.

  • “When thou passest through the waters, I with thee; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow thee: when thou walkest through the fire, thou shalt not be burned; neither shall the flame kindle upon thee.”
    Isaiah 43:2 · KJV

    Guided prayer. In all trials, God, I trust You to be with me. Through waters, fires, and rivers, protect and preserve me. Amen.

  • “And he said unto me, My grace is sufficient for thee: for my strength is made perfect in weakness. Most gladly therefore will I rather glory in my infirmities, that the power of Christ may rest upon me.”
    2 Corinthians 12:9 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Your grace is sufficient, Lord. In my weakness, Your power is perfected. Let me boast in my weaknesses, that Your strength may rest upon me. Amen.

  • “And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to purpose.”
    Romans 8:28 · KJV

    Guided prayer. God, work all things together for good in my life, as I love You and am called according to Your purpose. Help me to see Your hand in everything. Amen.

  • “[To the chief Musician for the sons of Korah, A Song upon Alamoth.] God our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble.”
    Psalms 46:1 · KJV

    Guided prayer. God, my refuge and strength, an ever-present help in trouble, I rely on Your unwavering support. Keep me steadfast. Amen.

  • “Teaching them to observe all things whatsoever I have commanded you: and, lo, I am with you alway, unto the end of the world. Amen.”
    Matthew 28:20 · KJV

    Guided prayer. Jesus, thank You for Your promise to be with me always. Your presence gives me courage and peace. Amen.

  • “These things I have spoken unto you, that in me ye might have peace. In the world ye shall have tribulation: but be of good cheer; I have overcome the world.”
    John 16:33 · KJV

    Guided prayer. In You, Jesus, I find peace amidst the worldâs troubles. Help me to take heart, knowing You have overcome the world. Amen.

Reading these in context

A verse lifted out of its chapter can be made to say almost anything. Bible Project is built so that a topic list is a way in to the text rather than a substitute for it: tap a verse and you land in the reader at that exact verse, with the surrounding chapter there to be read.

If a sentence is dense or archaic — and the King James Version has plenty of both — you can ask for a plain-language explanation of that verse without leaving the page, then keep asking follow-up questions in the same thread until the answer actually lands. That is Father AI, and it is the one part of the app that needs a connection.

When you are running low and offline

The whole point of carrying both translations on the device is that the moment you need them is rarely the moment you have signal. Reading, keyword search, the topic collections, verse compare and the verse of the day all work with the network off entirely.
